## Ongoing contract

- Same daily scheduler, **08:00 Asia/Hong_Kong**. No required Telegram paste.
  Scheduler and bot recovery share one serialized date-owned publishing claim.
- At least four **verified** Specials per ISO week, with catch-up days and
  bounded retry. Configuration and simulated cadence tests do not prove that
  future weekly quotas have already been met.
- Every completed Special enters an idempotent source-hash-keyed Binary job:
  BUILD / INVEST / JOY story, four native editions, 6–12 reviewed carousel pages,
  live-file verification, then planning records. Owner articles keep their slots.
- Monthly preference review and weekly Intelligence work use actual supported
  conversation reads and available memory. Coverage and truncation are recorded
  privately; this is not merged-memory or full-transcript reconstruction.
- BTC Probability Atlas and HK IPO Blind Box are under Asymmetry, with permanent
  redirects from their former doors. Their financial workflows remain manual
  until a separately evidenced and authorized first loop.

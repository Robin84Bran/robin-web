# AIDC 101-1 — Electricity to Intelligence

Lesson route: https://iamrobin.ai/intelligence/aidc101/101-1/

Entry point: https://iamrobin.ai/intelligence/aidc101/

The existing construction-readiness model links to this first numbered lesson.
The 64-second, eight-scene animation follows generation, deliverable power,
GPU compute, liquid cooling, orchestration, useful AI outputs, economic value,
and the final value chain. Equipment and activity are illustrative, not measurements.

## Source

- `src/pages/intelligence/aidc101/101-1.astro`: route and learning-resource metadata.
- `src/components/intelligence/AidcFactory.astro`: accessible lesson and player markup.
- `public/intelligence/aidc101/101-1/app.js`: dependency-free WebGL animation.
- `public/intelligence/aidc101/101-1/i18n.js`: English, simplified Chinese,
  traditional Chinese and Japanese captions, scene signs, controls and summaries.
- `public/intelligence/aidc101/101-1/styles.css`: shared Robin typography and
  moon/gold page treatment around the dark cinematic scene.

English is the default. `?lang=zh-Hans`, `?lang=zh-Hant` and `?lang=ja`
deep-link into the other languages. Switching language retains the playback
position. Links between the overview and lesson carry the selected language.
Reduced-motion preferences pause initial playback. A readable lesson summary
remains available when WebGL cannot run.

## Validation

Astro checks/build and the repository's release checks apply. The new route is
included in the sitemap filter and SEO allowlist. Four-language DOM checks cover
captions, all ten scene signs, the final chain, playback-position retention,
navigation and WebGL fallback. These checks do not certify actual GPU rendering
on every browser or device.

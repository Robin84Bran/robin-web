# Signal 6 — AI infrastructure

Effective November 5, 2026, the sixth Daily Signal is **AI infrastructure**.
Its scope includes power, interconnection, land, cooling, networking,
construction, equipment, financing, utilization, operating economics and
capital-project delivery. Career opportunities and job searches belong to
the separate Seeker workflow.

Use the corresponding infrastructure-only label in all four Daily Briefing
and Action Flow editions. The eight-slot order is unchanged. Earlier source
captures remain historical evidence; the November 5 label correction does
not change the selected story, facts or action.

# Three gauges, one dated contraction

Published: 9 November 2026  
Source signal: 3  
Research cutoff: 9 November 2026, 09:11 HKT  
Scope: A public-source comparison of two listed-product flow groups and one stablecoin circulation estimate. Completing this research did not execute or authorize a trade, transfer, allocation or portfolio decision.

## Question

The selected research lead proposed waiting for weekly Bitcoin and Ether exchange-traded-product flows and an aligned seven-day stablecoin measure to stop contracting before considering a return of capital. This artifact asks what the latest complete window shows and how far that observation can reasonably travel.

## Method

- Add the five complete Farside daily totals for U.S.-listed Bitcoin products from 2–6 November 2026.
- Add the matching five daily totals for Ether products.
- Compare DefiLlama's `totalCirculatingUSD.peggedUSD` endpoints for 1 and 8 November, a seven-calendar-day interval.
- Keep the listed-product flows and stablecoin stock measure separate. Their directional agreement is a breadth observation; their values are not combined.
- Compare the result with the 31 October Special, whose complete window had Bitcoin-product inflows alongside Ether-product outflows and stablecoin contraction.

## What changed

The 31 October comparison showed different directions: Bitcoin-product flows were positive, Ether-product flows were negative, and the stablecoin estimate contracted. In the latest window, all three selected readings contracted.

The proposed condition therefore remained uncleared at the research cutoff. That is a dated description of a chosen rule. It does not establish that the rule improves investment outcomes, forecast prices, identify investor motives or show the same capital moving among these channels.

## Limits

Farside describes its tables as automatically updated and warns that they may contain errors or inaccuracies. This artifact checks the displayed rows and arithmetic; it does not rebuild every issuer record independently.

DefiLlama's series is an estimated circulating dollar value across tracked assets. Coverage and historical classifications can change. It is an indirect view of liquidity rather than a measure of cash available on exchanges or capital committed to risk assets.

The product totals cover five U.S. trading sessions. The stablecoin comparison spans seven calendar days. One complete week establishes a current observation, while persistence, causality and predictive value require evidence across further periods.

# Frozen experiment — Your Queue or Mine?

Frozen before batch evaluation on 2026-11-23. Four scripted policies receive
the same jobs, owners, deadlines and four-worker action budget: stay in lane,
always help the longest queue, help only a queue above a threshold, or choose a
non-empty queue at random. A handoff can add zero to twelve actions.

Report completions, expiries, transfers, transfer actions, idle actions and
unfinished work separately. Promotion requires exact replay across 128 seeds
and sensitivity settings rather than a universal winner. This is a synthetic
queue, not a study of people, AI models, organisms or financial systems. An
empirical study is not part of this release.

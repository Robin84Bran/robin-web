The megawatt had a deadline.

A planned 23,000-GPU supercomputer at Loughton carried a Q1 2027 customer window. The reported grid-power horizon moved into the early-to-mid 2030s. Microsoft withdrew.

That is more than a delay. A megawatt arriving after the customer's planning window can be a different product.

AI capacity is not just a GPU count or a power number. It is power, place and time arriving together.

The useful sequence is:
announced → contracted → powered → commissioned → revenue-producing.

Each arrow needs its own evidence. A signature does not energize a rack.

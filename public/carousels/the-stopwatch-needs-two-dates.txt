A stopwatch needs two dates.

Torus says Foundry can move from contract to running AI workloads in 12–16 weeks. The design is plausible: factory assembly can compress the work visible at a data-center site.

But the public materials use two starting labels—contract to token and dirt to token—and the accepted finish changes across Foundry's three commercial models.

That matters because first workload, reliable service, utilization and revenue are different events.

The smallest useful next proof is a customer delivery receipt: one deployment, one commercial model, one dated start and one dated accepted finish. A later operating window can then test reliability and economics.

The system may compress the build. The evidence still needs to start the clock.

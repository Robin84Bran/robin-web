OpenAI's healthcare configurations can search a dedicated medical-source index. That changes the shelf. It does not fill the scorecard.

The key design problem appears when a Sol-first workflow adds Astra review. If Astra searches again, two things changed: the reader and the retrieval opportunity.

So measure two arms:

1. Astra reviews the same frozen source pack.
2. Astra reviews and may search again, with every new source and call recorded.

Then keep every material claim in the ledger and compare total workflow cost per source-verified correct claim.

A better shelf is an input. Accuracy is an outcome.

The five demonstrations came last.

NVIDIA reports that GR00T 2 reached 95% average success on Dexter-50 after five target-robot demonstrations per task. Those demonstrations took less than three minutes of robot data.

But the model arrived after 436,414 reported hours of human video, handheld-gripper demonstrations and robot teleoperation. And the 50 benchmark tasks were selected from tasks that reached at least 80% after five episodes.

That does not make the result uninteresting. It makes the mechanism clearer.

The short lesson adapted a broad prior to a new body. It did not teach the task from scratch.

The useful diligence question is not only “How many demonstrations?” It is “What had already been learned before the count began?”

Google's highest robot-progress band spans 80% through 100%.

That creates a useful contradiction: almost finished and finished can share one label.

Gemini Robotics ER 2 makes progress observable. Google says a system can use that estimate to adjust an action or retry one failed step instead of restarting the whole sequence.

But the published numbers answer narrower questions:

57.4%: five-band progress classification
91.3%: moment finding in video
0.96 seconds: mean absolute distance for the reported moment-finding benchmark

None is an accepted customer-job completion rate.

The missing layer is an external acceptance receipt: a human-defined final-state check applied after the model judgment.

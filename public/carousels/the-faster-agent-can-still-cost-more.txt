A faster agent can still be the expensive one.

Shield reports GPT-6 Astra was 35–40% faster on average than its earlier computer-use models, with fewer failures, fewer timeouts and less technician effort. Those are promising company-reported signals.

The operating question begins one step later: what did the whole system spend to produce work that passed an independent final-state check?

Cost per accepted completion = model + browser/runtime + active technician cost, divided by accepted autonomous completions.

That denominator keeps retries, human rescues, safe stops, false completions and failures visible. If accepted outcomes are zero, the ratio is unavailable; the spend does not disappear.

The proposed 100-run study has not been executed. It is a measurement design, not a result.

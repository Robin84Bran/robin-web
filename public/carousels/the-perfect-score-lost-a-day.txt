The dashboard says 100%. The calendar says a day is missing.

If reliability begins with recorded invocations, an expected run that never appears can vanish from the denominator.

In a purely illustrative month:

29 delivered / 29 recorded = 100% transport
28 accepted / 30 expected = 93.3% outcome

Both rates are honest. They answer different questions.

The repair is an expected-occurrence ledger created before work begins, then joined forward to invocation, session, root turn, artifact and acceptance. Keep one occurrence identity and separate attempt identities for retries.

Green is useful. But green begins too late.

The driver can leave the cab without human labor leaving the mile.

Kodiak AI announced an agreement for DTL Transport to deploy its system in 75 DTL-owned and DTL-operated trucks, paying by the mile. The metal sits with the carrier.

The support layer does not disappear. Kodiak's filing describes integration, remote monitoring, operational support and remote support as part of Driver-as-a-Service.

That creates a cleaner investor question:

provider gross profit per accepted paid mile = (revenue − full provider cost) / accepted paid miles

Count the assists, their duration, unavailable hours, field recoveries, maintenance and idle time. Then ask whether accepted mileage grows faster than the support cost around it.

The empty cab is the visible breakthrough. The offboard ledger decides whether it compounds.

Three down arrows are not one number.

In the latest complete window:

• Bitcoin-product flows: -$1.282bn
• Ether-product flows: -$0.365bn
• tracked USD-stablecoin value: -$5.148bn / -1.7253%

All three gauges contracted. But only the first two can be added. They are aligned five-session flow estimates. The stablecoin reading is a change in an estimated stock across seven calendar days.

A pipe meter and a reservoir gauge can agree on direction without measuring the same water.

The useful result is breadth, not an invented $6.8bn total. And even that breadth result is dated: one complete window does not prove causality, predict prices or validate an investment rule.

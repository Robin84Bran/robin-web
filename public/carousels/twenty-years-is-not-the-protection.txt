A 20-year PPA gives a $4 billion power project a buyer and a revenue horizon.

It does not reveal who pays when reality disagrees with the model.

Four private clauses can change the economics without changing the announced megawatts:
1. delay — who finances the wait;
2. fuel — who absorbs the index and transport;
3. replacement — who pays after service or warranty; and
4. exit — who carries termination and residual-value risk.

The public record supports the buyer, ownership, term and equipment commitment. It does not yet price every downside path.

Read the remedies before the megawatts.

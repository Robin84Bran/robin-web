USDY began counting accrued value by the second.

A one-token quote still appeared frozen.

In a read-only boundary simulation, the high-precision USDY price rose by about $0.000000001345 across one second. The gross quote before and after remained 1.152259 USDC because the conversion ended at six decimals and rounded down.

The contradiction disappears when the clocks are separated:

• economic value accumulates
• the oracle calculates at request time
• the display moves only after its next rounded unit

This was a frozen-state calculation, not a redemption. Fees, eligibility, liquidity, service availability, settlement and realized proceeds remain unmeasured.

When a financial number looks frozen, ask which clock you are actually watching.

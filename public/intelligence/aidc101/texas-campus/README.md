# From Megawatts to Compute | Texas campus v0.1
Illustrative underwriting experiment, prepared 2026-11-10. No identified site, executed contract, tariff quote or calibrated scenario probabilities. Files include an editable input JSON, a standard-library Python script, summary output and a full hourly dispatch ledger. Run python3 simulate.py from any directory. All monetary values are assumed constant USD.

## Boundary
Calendar 2027, 365 days, fixed Central Standard Time, 1-hour steps. All scenarios request 60 facility MW for 20 hours and 100 MW from 16:00 through 19:59 inclusive, every day from January 1. Demand is requested even while the site is dark. PUE 1.25 maps facility energy to IT energy. Annual IT demand = 467,200 MWh. Cooling, network, deployed IT and commissioning are assumed ready whenever power is deliverable. The Jan 31 deadline metric is the minimum IT MW actually served during its four peak hours. It is not a continuous 24/7 capacity accreditation. No on-site generation, black start, degradation or forced outages.

## Physical paths
All supply enters one 100 MW substation. Grid service starts at 80 MW; ordinary expansion adds 20 MW Jan 15. In the supply-delay case that expansion slips 90 days to Apr 15. The firm alternative retains 80 MW of grid rights and replaces the expansion with a *physically deliverable*, independently reserved block of 10 MW Jan 1 plus 10 MW Jan 15. A financial PPA alone cannot do this. In the common-gate case only the substation moves, to March 2. It blocks grid, contracted supply and battery charging until then. Baseline supply project calendars continue independently of substation readiness. Each stress is applied separately.
Battery starts empty. AC rating 20 MW, 80 MWh deliverable, round-trip efficiency 85%, all losses booked at charging. Charge off peak within meter and import headroom, discharge during peak to avoid high-priced purchases or meet constrained load. Full refill requires 94.12 MWh from the grid. The same deterministic dispatch applies throughout. Peak-price shape is known, no hindsight optimization.

## Costs
Synthetic peak/offpeak wholesale prices = 163.32/45 USD per MWh. Their time-weighted average is 64.72, numerically anchored to the 2023 ERCOT Houston-zone annual load-weighted real-time figure. These weighting conventions differ. Neither the hourly price shape nor the mean paid by this campus is a historical replay. Source: https://www.potomaceconomics.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2023-State-of-the-Market-Report_Final_060624.pdf (Table 1). Data source for a later chronological replay: https://www.ercot.com/mktinfo/prices
Assumed 12 USD/MWh delivery adder applies to actual imports including grid charging. Firm supply is take-or-pay at 75 USD/MWh on every available contract block even when the campus cannot receive power; zero resale or damages recovery. No separate export, demand charge, ancillary revenues, taxes, escalation or lost-compute penalty. Battery efficiency benchmark: https://atb.nlr.gov/electricity/2025/2023/utility-scale_battery_storage
Illustrative owner commitments all signed before Jan 1: common electrical works 20m USD; battery incremental 24m USD; alternatively, firm connection incremental 5m USD. The utility expansion has no additional campus-funded contribution in this case. Capital committed is the signed power-infrastructure capex, excluding IT/building capital, financing carry, recurring energy obligations and refundable collateral. No staged capex commitments are assumed, hence the same capital exposure across delays for a given portfolio.
The annual modeled delivered power cost includes wholesale or contracted supply, delivery, annualized infrastructure and incremental battery O&M. CRF at 8% over 20 years for fixed works, 15 years for storage. Full annual fixed charge in every case; battery O&M 2% of battery capex per year. Divide by IT MWh actually served. A lower bill driven by lost service is not a benefit. Monetize compute opportunity cost separately if a supported margin per IT MWh becomes available.

## Audits and next evidence
Hourly meter balance, battery state limits, and annual demand reconciliation are checkable in hourly.csv. On-time grid shortfall = 14 days × 4 h × 20 MW / 1.25. Grid-upgrade-delay shortfall = 104 days × 4 h × 20 MW / 1.25. Common outage = 60 days × 1,280 IT MWh/day. Check which rights are independent against a site one-line diagram, signed utility service agreement and delivery-point terms. Calibrate scenario weights only after site-specific evidence. Historical test input, tariff and capital quotes must be versioned separately.

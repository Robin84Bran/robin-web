# Frozen experiment — Checkpoint or Restart?

Frozen before batch evaluation on 2026-11-16. Four scripted policies receive
the same urgent arrivals and one action per tick: finish first, discard and
restart, checkpoint and return, or a seeded coin flip. The batch retains seeds
1–128 across default, quiet, storm, save-cost, deadline and job-length settings.

Report completed value, urgent completions and expiries, discarded progress,
checkpoint actions and unfinished work separately. Promotion requires exact
replay and sensitivity rather than one universal winner. This is a synthetic
queue, not a study of people, AI models, organisms or financial systems. An
empirical study is not part of this release.

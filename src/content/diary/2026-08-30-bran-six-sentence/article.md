Anything can become a six-sentence paragraph.
Just:
1. What is it?
2. What does it do?
3. What does it look like?
4. How is it different?
5. Why is it important?
6. What did I learn?

That’s works for literally everything.Robot vacuum.  Tornado.  Penguins. Karate class. Roblox. Disney movie. A trip to Macau. Even Bitcoin.🤣

The deeper pattern is: Reading → Observation → Questions → Discussion → Paragraph

Most schools stop at Reading → Worksheet. Now let’s add the middle layers where thinking happens.

After a discussion wanders through robot vacuums, AI, autonomy, purpose, life goals, and future plans, Bran has to answer:

“Okay, what are the six most important sentences?”

That is exactly the same cognitive skill behind writing essays, giving presentations, investing, engineering design and executive decision-making. 

10,000 observations to compress into 6 sentences. 🤣🍷

Step 1: Notice
What caught my attention?

Step 2: Compare
How is it different?

Step 3: Question
Why does it work this way?

Step 4: Discuss
What does it remind me of?

Step 5: Abstract
What is the bigger idea?

Step 6: Explain
Can I write it clearly?

The same process to build a complex quant lab, just with fewer penguins and more spreadsheets.

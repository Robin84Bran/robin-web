## 1. Frontier models and agents: cheap subagents change the autonomy budget

Date: October 7, 2026

**Fact:** Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5. For prompts up to 100,000 tokens, input and output cost $0.10 and $0.50 per million tokens, one tenth of Haiku 4.5's price; Sonnet 5.5 cache reads also fell by half to $0.10 per million tokens. Anthropic positions Haiku for high-volume narrow work such as retrieval, summarization, classification, routing and subagents, while reserving complex coding for stronger models. Performance figures remain vendor or customer evaluations, not proof of unattended long-horizon autonomy. [Anthropic launch](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5) [Anthropic newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news)

**Inference:** The important change is the price of retries, parallel search and criticism loops. Recovery and acceptance still set the autonomy ceiling; the cheaper model lets a system buy more redundancy.

**Why Robin should care:** A one-person company should not assign its most expensive model to every step. The real unit economics are total cost per accepted outcome, human-rescue minutes and failure-recovery rate.

**One Action:** Freeze acceptance criteria across 50 real RobinOS tasks and compare a strong-model-only route with Haiku subagents under a strong planner and critic. Do not promote the route without the same denominator.

*Model/API watch: the independent sweep also covered OpenAI and GPT‑6 Astra successors, Google/Gemini, xAI/Grok, Meta, and Chinese/open models including DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi and GLM. Google introduced an enterprise Gemini agent on October 8; OpenAI's Jump Trading case still makes a secure environment and human judgment explicit. No other update changed this slot's operating choice as directly as Haiku's subagent economics. [Google Gemini agent](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-at-work/) [OpenAI Jump Trading](https://openai.com/index/jump-trading/)*

## 2. Physical AI: humanoid labor finally has an auditable cost stack

Date: October 6, 2026

**Fact:** Agility Robotics' investor-day materials say Digit has accumulated more than 65,000 hours of production experience and describe two adoption models. The ownership model could generate roughly $400,000 of lifetime revenue for Agility over five years; RaaS could generate about $500,000. The company also reports nine committed facilities and more than $300 million of Digit 5 committed orders, while stating that those orders depend on deployment milestones and are not current-period revenue. The economics are illustrative management assumptions. [Agility investor relations](https://www.agilityrobotics.com/investors) [SEC investor presentation](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2074973/000121390026107261/ea030784201ex99-1.htm)

**Inference:** Humanoid comparisons are moving from dancing videos toward fully loaded cost per successful action. Hardware price is only the first layer. Deployment, software, maintenance, downtime, intervention, failed actions and contract realization determine labor economics.

**Why Robin should care:** The US–China contest may split into cheaper Chinese bodies and more deployable US systems. Both need the same task, utilization and failure definitions.

 **One Action:** Apply one hurdle to every humanoid: five-year fully loaded cost per successful tote move at 80% productive utilization, with intervention and downtime as separate denominators.

[LS Power announcement](https://www.lspower.com/news/ls-power-closes-6-billion-fund-vi/) [Google nuclear collaboration](https://blog.google/company-news/why-were-backing-americas-existing-nuclear-plants/)

**Inference:** Value may accrue across generation, transmission, storage, capacity markets and long-term procurement contracts. The winner need not be the data-center landowner; it must deliver reliable megawatts into a constrained node under a financeable agreement.

**Why Robin should care:** This is a career and capital-research intersection. A credible work sample needs to explain cash flow and risk, rather than repeat an AI-power shortage narrative.

**One Action:** Build a one-page research-only underwriting card separating deliverable MW, node, capacity revenue, fuel hedge, contracted load, upgrade capex, permit status and exit dependence. Keep missing fields UNKNOWN.

## 7. Late-stage private markets: escaping the Nvidia tax may create an architecture-lock-in tax

Date: October 5, 2026

**Fact:** TechCrunch reports that Etched is fielding early investment offers at $40–50 billion after raising $700 million at a $21 billion valuation in September. The company says it delivered an early system to lead investor Jane Street and has more than $1 billion of customer orders. No new round or terms are confirmed; revenue, stable runtime and performance lack independent validation. [TechCrunch report](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/etched-weighs-investment-offers-at-40-billion-to-50-billion-valuations/) [Etched systems](https://www.etched.com/)

**Private-market judgment: WATCH.** This is not an accessible opportunity and authorizes no outreach or investment. The $40–50 billion range is an early market signal, not a completed financing.

**Inference:** Transformer specialization can reduce the general-purpose GPU tax while adding architecture-change, software-migration, supply-chain, utilization and customer-concentration risk. Capital is absorbing most of that technical risk before broad commercial rack evidence exists.

**Why Robin should care:** Avoiding a chip-company tax is never free. Each substitute creates a migration, utilization or lock-in tax.

**One Action:** Keep WATCH and seek no shares. Reopen only after independent disclosure of throughput per watt, stable operating hours, recognized revenue, customer concentration and migration cost.

[PLTR price history](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pltr/history/) [QQQ price history](https://chartexchange.com/symbol/nasdaq-qqq/historical/)

**Inference:** The outperformance reflects a software narrative and analyst rerating, rather than synchronized improvement across the AI capital cycle. Price discovery is real; the causal story still needs the next contract, revenue and free-cash-flow evidence.

**Why Robin should care:** Markets are rewarding companies that translate AI into customer workflows while demanding better financing evidence from hardware and infrastructure. Those value-capture models should not share one multiple.

**One Action:** Do not chase the weekly move. Validate the rerating only with new contract value, revenue growth, gross margin and free cash flow, not an analyst price target.

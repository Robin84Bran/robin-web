## 1. Frontier Models and Agents | Reusable instructions reach the workspace

Date: September 30 announcement; Workspace Rapid Release rollout begins October 5, 2026.

**Fact:** Google says Workspace skills use Markdown-native SKILL.md instructions and can be stacked in a conversation. Rapid Release rollout is scheduled for October 5–12; Scheduled Release starts October 19, and Gemini app rollout starts October 13. Skills currently do not sync between Workspace and the Gemini app. [google](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/09/skills-gemini-app-workspace.html)

**Inference:** This creates a practical path for reusable operating instructions, with a separate copy and review burden across surfaces. Rollout dates do not prove availability in a particular account or better task completion.

**Why Robin should care:** A repeatable editorial or vendor-review procedure is more useful than another model ranking when its permissions and acceptance rules survive reuse.

**One Action:** Prepare one redacted skill portability card with a fixed input, expected output, permissions and version hash; test each surface only when account access is available.

It reports eightfold revenue growth over twelve months and more than 1,000 company customers, and plans to expand development infrastructure and its Mac hardware fleet. [namespace](https://namespace.so/blog/series-b)

**Inference:** Build and test infrastructure can capture value as agents generate more code. Growth multiples without starting revenue, margins or retention cannot establish attractive unit economics; a strategic infrastructure acquisition is an analytical exit scenario, not an announced deal.

**One Action:** Classify Namespace as INVESTIGATE and save a public-evidence checklist for absolute revenue, retention, gross margin, hardware utilization and deal access before any investment assessment.

## 8. Public Equities and AI Themes | TeraWulf doubles contracted power, not live capacity

Date: Agreements executed October 1; Form 8-K filed October 5, 2026.

**Fact:** TeraWulf’s filing increases Muskie contracted power from 500 MW to 1 GW. The first 500 MW still targets a 2028 ramp; the second moves from 2030 to 2029, subject to Kentucky regulatory approval and the utility’s construction schedule. It also seeks more secured financing, which is not assured. [terawulf](https://investors.terawulf.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-064757/wulf-20261001.htm)

**Inference:** The value driver is conversion of a conditional power contract into financed, delivered and leased capacity. No energized megawatts, incremental revenue or price-performance conclusion follows from the contract alone.

**Why Robin should care:** The listed-equity thesis can be tested against an explicit sequence of approvals, financing, construction and customer obligations.

**One Action:** Save a Muskie milestone bridge with separate fields for approval, debt close, energized capacity and contracted revenue; leave missing outcomes blank until original filings establish them.

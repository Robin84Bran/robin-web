## 1. Frontier models and agents｜GPT-6.1 Terra pushes computer use into a lower-cost tier

Date: October 26, 2026.

**Fact:** OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. Standard API pricing is $1 per million input tokens, $0.05 per million cached input tokens and $5 per million output tokens; OpenAI says standard prices are 50% below GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol. Its visual, computer-use, coding and factuality results are OpenAI evaluations, not direct evidence of RobinOS task outcomes. [Product announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-terra/) | [Model release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes)

**Inference:** A faster, cheaper computer-use model expands the economic boundary of automation. Token price is still different from cost per accepted outcome.

**Why Robin should care:** RobinOS contains many browser, research and file workflows. The useful question is whether Terra can finish the same replayable tasks with less intervention, not whether it wins one vendor benchmark.

**One Action:** Add ten read-only, replayable Terra shadow tasks to Model Olympics and record acceptance rate, human minutes, recoveries, end-to-end duration and cost per accepted outcome; do not change production model authority.

## 2. Physical AI and robotics｜Robots start moving part of the brain to the network edge

Date: October 27, 2026.

**Fact:** T-Mobile says it is evaluating whether parts of Figure's Helix S1 and S2 models can run closer to the network edge; exploring an edge supervision layer and conversational AI with Serve Robotics; and building the first instances of Kinetic Edge with AWS. It also names traffic-sensing exploration with San Jose, LinkerVision and Laplace Intelligence. The company does not disclose production latency, handoff failures, intervention reduction, uptime or commercial terms. [T-Mobile announcement](https://www.t-mobile.com/news/network/physical-ai-is-leaving-the-lab)

**Inference:** Physical AI competition may shift from how much compute fits inside a robot to how device, edge and cloud divide work. This remains architecture and trial progress, not proof of scaled operations.

**Why Robin should care:** Safe offload could change robot weight, heat and battery constraints. Network loss, tail latency and human takeover then become new system boundaries.

**One Action:** Create a read-only acceptance card for the same task in local and edge modes, recording P50/P99 latency, disconnected safety state, recovery time, human takeover and task cost; keep WATCH until measured.

**One Action:** Keep HOLD. Upgrade broad capital entry to provisional re-entry only after three complete sessions with positive combined BTC and ETH ETF flows and expanding seven-day stablecoin supply; no trade.

## 4. Stablecoins and payment rails｜The OCC finalizes the line between stablecoins and tokenized deposits

Date: October 23, 2026; effective January 18, 2027.

**Fact:** The US Office of the Comptroller of the Currency issued its final rule implementing the GENIUS Act for OCC-supervised permitted payment stablecoin issuers, including reserve, custody, capital and supervisory requirements. A deposit remains outside the definition of payment stablecoin even when recorded on distributed-ledger technology, and the stablecoin yield prohibition does not apply to deposit interest. The OCC removed the proposed rebuttable presumption while retaining authority to address evasion. [Federal Register final rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-23/html/2026-21706.htm)

**Inference:** Beyond a product label, issuer identity, legal claim, reserves, yield source and regulator determine the economic model of stablecoins and tokenized deposits.

**Why Robin should care:** Robin's payments experience can separate responsibility and cash flow inside “on-chain dollars” instead of treating every token rail as the same product.

**One Action:** Build a one-page rule map for payment stablecoins, tokenized deposits and ordinary deposits across issuer, claim, reserves, yield, redemption and regulator; mark fields not directly supported by the rule UNKNOWN.

## 5. Content and distribution｜The slowest step owns the agent

Date: October 28, 2026.

**Fact:** GPT-6.1 Terra pushes computer use into a lower price tier; Cerebras and Oracle plan to bring fast inference to OCI in 2027; NVIDIA and Oracle say CPU work between model calls, including tool execution, code and data retrieval, can leave GPUs waiting. The three sources provide no shared end-to-end task benchmark and do not prove that any combination has reduced RobinOS cost per accepted outcome. [Terra](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-terra/) | [Cerebras and Oracle](https://cerebras.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/cerebras-and-oracle-team-bring-cerebras-ai-inference-oracle) | [NVIDIA and Oracle](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-oracle-expand-nvidia-vera-rubin-to-multi-gigawatt-scale-and-bring-nvidia-vera-cpu-to-oracle-cloud-infrastructure)

**Inference:** Agent speed is a system property. The slowest part among inference, tools, browser, storage, approvals, retries and recovery can consume the gains from a faster model.

**Why Robin should care:** Robin can own a more useful frame than a model leaderboard: total waiting time per accepted outcome, with AI infrastructure and the real workflow drawn on the same page.

**One Action:** Publish *The Slowest Step Owns the Agent* in the English canonical and three localized editions, using one replayable workflow to show a latency waterfall across model, tools, data, human review and recovery, and archive one LinkedIn derivative.

## 6. AI infrastructure｜Oracle and NVIDIA take Vera Rubin toward a multi-gigawatt plan

Date: October 27, 2026.

**Fact:** NVIDIA and Oracle announced general availability of Vera Rubin NVL72 and Vera CPUs on OCI and plans to expand OCI Superclusters to multi-gigawatt scale; OCI also plans to introduce HGX Rubin NVL8. The release says OpenAI has trained and served GPT-6 Astra on an OCI Supercluster in Abilene with more than 100,000 Blackwell GPUs. The multi-gigawatt expansion, NVL8, delivered power, utilization, capex and customer economics remain planned or undisclosed. [NVIDIA announcement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-oracle-expand-nvidia-vera-rubin-to-multi-gigawatt-scale-and-bring-nvidia-vera-cpu-to-oracle-cloud-infrastructure)

**Inference:** The compute bottleneck is becoming coordinated delivery of GPU, CPU, network, power, cooling and sandbox capacity. A multi-gigawatt target is not delivered capacity.

**Why Robin should care:** AI-infrastructure research should record generally available products, planned capacity, energized capacity, customer acceptance and revenue separately.

**One Action:** Add an OCI delivery card to the AI Infrastructure ledger with separate fields for Vera GA, planned gigawatts, energized MW, accepted clusters, utilization and contracted cash flow; keep the last four UNKNOWN until evidenced.

Guidewire says hx software supports underwriting decisions across more than $75 billion of annual commercial P&C premium. Closing conditions remain; hx revenue, margin, retention, purchase-price allocation and the contingent thresholds are undisclosed. [Guidewire announcement](https://guidewire.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/guidewire-signs-definitive-agreement-acquire-hyperexponential)

**Inference:** This is more than an AI label. The buyer is paying cash for an existing workflow and tying additional value to recurring revenue, while the operating variables needed to price the exit remain hidden.

**Why Robin should care:** Exit quality for late-stage software and AI companies should be decomposed into revenue quality, retention, margin and post-integration customer economics.

**One Action:** Keep WATCH. Recalculate the exit revenue multiple only after closing and disclosure of hx revenue, net retention, gross margin, customer concentration, contingent ARR thresholds and integration cost.

## 1. Frontier Models and Agents | Project Swap tests whether an agent knows what its owner wants

Date: Research published September 24, 2026; experiment conducted during the summer, exact experiment day undisclosed. [Anthropic research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-swap).

**Fact:** Anthropic reports a controlled book-swap experiment with 201 employees. After a short intake, the model's preferences agreed with participants on 61% of ranked book pairs; the study identifies imperfect knowledge of preferences as an important limit. These are the lab's experimental results, not a general accuracy guarantee.

**Inference:** Strong negotiation can optimize an inaccurate picture of the owner's wishes.

**Why Robin should care:** An autonomous system needs a test of preference fidelity alongside execution quality.

**One Action:** Specify a ten-choice synthetic preference test, with owner rankings hidden from the agent and pairwise agreement as the acceptance metric; keep execution proposed and grant no transaction authority.

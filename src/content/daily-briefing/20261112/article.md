**Why Robin should care — interpretation:** Discovery is extending into business identity and conversational entry points inside AI. ## 2. Physical AI and robotics｜Hospital logistics moves from one handoff to a 50-robot framework

Date: November 11, 2026.

**Fact:** Nasdaq-listed Arrive AI announced a $100,000 SAFE investment in Ottonomy and an agreement contemplating the deployment of 50 autonomous ground robots. The companies say the combination builds on existing specimen-delivery operations at Hancock Regional Hospital. “Contemplating” does not mean 50 robots have been ordered, deployed or accepted. [Arrive AI release](https://www.newswire.com/news/arrive-ai-announces-strategic-investment-in-ottonomy-to-accelerate-22887472)

**Why Robin should care — interpretation:** The useful system combines robots, secure handoff points and hospital workflows. Quantity is a framework; takeover rate, on-time completion and cost per delivery determine whether expansion works.

**One Action:** Maintain a deployment ledger that counts scale only when each site discloses accepted robots, operating duration and human takeovers.

Date: ETFs use the complete November 10, 2026 session; stablecoins use November 11.

**Fact:** Farside’s completed tables show $95.0 million of net outflows from US spot BTC ETFs and $1.3 million from ETH ETFs. DefiLlama recorded aggregate stablecoin capitalization of $254.288 billion on November 11, down 0.75% over seven days. November 11 ETF rows remained incomplete when checked and were excluded rather than treated as zero. [Farside BTC](https://farside.co.uk/btc/) · [Farside ETH](https://farside.co.uk/eth/) · [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/stablecoins)

**Why Robin should care — interpretation:** One day of ETH pressure almost disappeared while BTC and stablecoin supply continued contracting. A near-zero day cannot establish a trend reversal.

**One Action:** Wait for the next complete session and record early stabilization only after at least two of BTC, ETH and stablecoin supply turn positive twice in succession.

## 8. Public equities｜AMD puts value per token on the table

Date: November 11, 2026.

**Fact:** AMD says AT&T processed more than one trillion tokens on AMD Instinct GPUs in Microsoft Azure and reported that its cache-aware AI gateway reduced costs by as much as 80%. AMD’s footnote explicitly says it did not test or verify third-party claims unless otherwise stated. [Original AMD article](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/ai-new-electricity-meter-running/)

**Why Robin should care — interpretation:** This frames a useful economic question: tokens are inputs and accepted business outcomes are outputs. Routing may lower cost, while success rate, latency and human recovery must be checked at the same time.

**One Action:** Compare a frozen task under single-model and cache-aware routing, recording total cost per accepted result, latency, failures and human minutes.

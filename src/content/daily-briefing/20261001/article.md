Thursday’s judgment: separate access, announcements and accepted work. A new brain, a hospital delivery and a reported quarter each cross a different threshold.

## 1. Frontier Models and Agents | Argon has a price before broad access

Date: September 30, 2026 announcement; checked October 1 HKT.

**Fact:** Google announced Gemini 4 Argon for selected trusted cyber defenders through Fairwind. Broader access is planned without a dated public launch in the announcement. Internal engineering gains are Google-reported, not our measured productivity. [google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/) · [fairwind](https://deepmind.google/fairwind-program/)

**Inference:** Access for a specific job is a separate variable from model capability. An unavailable model cannot yet carry that job’s delivery commitment.

**Why Robin should care:** RobinOS can prepare a fair comparison without changing its working model or permissions.

**One Action:** Save a twelve-case evaluation specification with fixed inputs, boundaries, deadlines, accepted outcomes, total costs and human interventions; leave execution pending supported access.

## 2. Physical AI and Robotics | Hospital robots must get through the door

Date: September 30, 2026 deployment announcement; checked October 1 HKT.

**Fact:** Relay Robotics announced two delivery robots, Dash and Daisy, at HCA Florida JFK Hospital. The company describes integration with elevators, authorized secure doors, life-safety systems and hospital IT. Site-specific operating economics and independently verified intervention rates were not established. [relay](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/relay-robotics-brings-autonomous-delivery-to-hca-florida-jfk-hospital-302893249.html)

**Inference:** The practical deployment unit includes the building and its controls. Vendor-wide reliability claims cannot substitute for this hospital’s measured results.

**Why Robin should care:** This offers a concrete way to examine what makes autonomy usable beyond a robot demonstration.

**One Action:** Save a hospital-delivery evidence card requiring completed trips, human handoffs, failed door or elevator interactions and staff minutes per delivery before estimating labor savings.

This is a customer benchmark reported by the supplier, not our independent replication or a completed-task comparison. **One Action:** Save a Rubin evidence card separating availability, benchmark conditions, accepted tasks, power, utilization and total cost; leave undisclosed deployment economics unresolved.

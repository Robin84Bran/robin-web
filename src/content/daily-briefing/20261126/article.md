The shared theme today is separation. Medical evidence work can separate low-cost search from expensive review. Lidar scale must separate shipment volume from field replacement costs. AI servers require separate ledgers for orders, backlog and recognized revenue. Growth becomes more legible once delivery costs stop hiding inside it.

## 1. Frontier Models and Agents | Medical evidence search gets its own route

Date: Nov 24.

**Fact:** OpenAI's [release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9624314-model-release-notes) confirm that API organizations can use Healthcare configurations through the Responses API without a ChatGPT for Healthcare workspace. With web search enabled, the models can search a dedicated medical index. At [official standard short-context rates](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing), GPT-6.2 Sol Healthcare costs **$2/$10 per million input/output tokens**, compared with **$8/$40 for GPT-6.2 Astra Healthcare**; promotional pricing lasts at least through Dec 31. Sol's model cost is one quarter of Astra's at identical token volumes. Total search, review and professional-judgment costs remain unknown, and OpenAI says these configurations do not replace professional medical judgment.

**Why Robin should care:** RobinOS can route original-trial retrieval separately from conflict review. The useful productivity measure is a correct claim supported by its source, rather than a cheap token.

**One Action:** Blind-test ten past clinical claims with Sol-only retrieval and Sol→Astra review. Choose the default route by total cost per correct claim verified against the original source.

## 2. Physical AI and Robotics | Field costs decide the economics after volume arrives

Date: Nov 25.

**Fact:** RoboSense's [HKEX filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1125/2026112501197.pdf) reports about **575,500 lidar units sold** in Q3, including about **145,200 for “robotics and others,” up 309% year over year**. The group also recognized an approximately **RMB118.7 million** replacement provision for one automotive OEM customer, while group gross margin fell from **23.9% to 3.7%**. The provision concerns the automotive customer and cannot be attributed to the robotics line.

**Why Robin should care:** Mass-production scale has hard numbers. One field-replacement provision was also large enough to alter a quarter's margin. A Physical AI moat needs both installation capacity and a clear boundary around after-sales responsibility.

**One Action:** In the next filing, check whether the provision is increased, reversed or used. Then update the estimated after-sales cost per installed unit.

## 3. Crypto Capital Flows | The reference signal exists; the public tables could not be reopened

Date: Nov 26 cutoff.

**Fact:** Today's reference listed Nov 24 US spot BTC and ETH ETF flows, but the [BTC table](https://farside.co.uk/BTC/) and [ETH table](https://farside.co.uk/ethereum-etf-flow-all-data/) both returned HTTP 403 during independent verification. That access failure does not prove the numbers wrong, and missing evidence is not zero. This edition therefore does not promote the reference's specific flow values as independently verified facts.

**Why Robin should care:** A fund-flow conclusion needs complete fund rows, a clear date and repeatable access. A polished total cannot replace a verifiable table.

**One Action:** Reopen the Nov 24 fund rows at the next accessible window. Accumulate three-day BTC flows excluding IBIT only after the fund-level rows are complete.

## 4. Stablecoins, FinTech and Token Rails | A bank entry point now feeds an onchain loan market

Date: Nov 25.

**Fact:** [Aave App](https://aave.com/blog/aave-app-launch) ended its waitlist and launched publicly on iOS and Android. Eligible users in supported US regions, the UK and the EEA can connect a bank account; the app allocates deposits across Aave lending markets and presents one balance. The official page advertises up to **7.25% variable APY** and says Aave is not a bank, funds lack government deposit insurance, and rates and eligibility vary by region.

**Why Robin should care:** Banks provide the entry point, a protocol matches credit and the app owns the customer relationship. Retention after promotions and realized risk costs will distinguish distribution from subsidy.

**One Action:** Track net retained balances after the first promotional periods end, excluding new deposits, and list any security loss or balance-protection payout separately.

## 5. Content and Visibility | When does the customer become the project manager?

Date: Nov 26.

**Fact:** Apple's [Quick Start documentation](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/102659) describes a verifiable fork: direct transfer keeps both devices occupied until it finishes, while restoring from iCloud downloads apps and data in the background. iamrobin.ai's public [Honest Agent research](https://iamrobin.ai/research/) and [handoff test archive](https://iamrobin.ai/ouroboros/202609/20260924/special/) supply another boundary: reporting an obstacle and delivering a feasible next step are different outcomes. The private oral recollection in today's reference is excluded from this edition's public fact base.

**Why Robin should care:** A service or agent can identify the obstacle correctly while handing coordination back to the customer. Completion should count the extra handoffs a customer must manage to reach the original goal.

**One Action:** Create “When the Customer Becomes the Project Manager”, using Apple's two public migration paths and the public Honest Agent handoff evidence to distinguish “reported obstacle” from “delivered feasible next step.” Add an anonymized reader-case table that records extra handoffs and steps that genuinely require human authorization.

## 6. AI Infrastructure | Construction progressed; energization evidence remains separate

Date: Nov 25.

**Fact:** A Sept 21 [Texas governor directive](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-directs-tceq-to-halt-data-center-permits) told TCEQ to halt data-center permits until ERCOT completed its audit. The Houston Chronicle later [reviewed 14 projects](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/business/energy/article/texas-data-center-permit-abbott-pause-construction-22538385.php) using regulatory records and satellite imagery and reported construction progress at at least **six** during the pause; some projects used permits outside the pause. Construction progress alone proves neither illegality nor an available energization date.

**Why Robin should care:** Civil work, permits and energization are separate evidence chains. Capacity begins to resemble a rent-producing asset only when an energization date becomes verifiable.

**One Action:** Split “under construction” in the Power Ledger into site work, applicable permit and verified energization date. Keep MW with an unknown third field out of the base revenue case.

## 7. Late-stage Private Markets | The first high-dose patient is still an early clinical milestone

Date: Nov 23.

**Fact:** Anocca's [clinical update](https://www.anocca.com/company/news/anocca-announces-dose-escalation-and-advances-multi-product-vidar-1-clinical-programme-in-pancreatic-cancer) says an independent safety monitoring committee reviewed low-dose safety and tolerability, approved escalation, and the first patient in the ANOC-001 high-dose cohort received treatment. VIDAR-1 is a multi-product umbrella trial for KRAS-mutated pancreatic cancer and plans to study up to 20 patients per product. The announcement disclosed no comparative objective-response or survival result. The company's approximately **SEK440 million 2025 financing** appears in an [older announcement](https://www.anocca.com/company/news/anocca-raises-sek-440-million-to-advance-clinical-trial-targeting-pancreatic-cancer); this clinical update is not a new financing round.

**Why Robin should care:** Dose escalation clears a safety gate. It does not answer efficacy, manufacturing throughput or commercial economics.

**One Action:** Wait for the first table showing enrollment, patients actually treated, serious adverse events and durable objective responses by dose before advancing diligence.

## 8. Public Equities | Dell's orders, backlog and revenue are three different promises

Date: Nov 24.

**Fact:** Dell's [SEC-filed earnings exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1571996/000157199626000050/exhibit991earnings8kq3fy27.htm) reports **$60.2 billion of Q3 AI orders**, **$132.3 billion of ending AI backlog**, and **$22.0 billion of AI-optimized server revenue**. The company says it vets customer quality and access to capital, land and power when taking orders. These figures describe new demand, unfulfilled commitments and recognized revenue; they cannot be added. Customer-level cash collection was not disclosed.

**Why Robin should care:** The supplier already treats financing, land and power as order-quality inputs. Evidence moves closer to cash as backlog becomes delivered revenue and then collected payment.

**One Action:** In the next filing, update a backlog→delivered revenue→cash collection bridge. Keep undisclosed AI-customer receipts marked unknown.

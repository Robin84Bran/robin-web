## 2. Physical AI | China directs humanoids toward military procurement, but 95% of shipments does not equal battlefield autonomy

Date: September 7, 2026｜Source: [Reuters review of more than 100 procurement notices, studies and patents](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/dance-floor-war-china-readies-humanoid-robots-combat-2026-09-07/)⁠

**Fact:** Reuters found that Chinese military institutions materially increased procurement and research involving humanoid perception, manipulation and training data during 2025–2026; BofA estimates Chinese manufacturers supplied roughly 95% of global humanoid shipments in 2025. There is no evidence that armed humanoids are operationally deployed, while published work places some urban-combat applications five to ten years away and still identifies endurance, reliability and human control as constraints.

**Inference:** China’s low-cost bodies, actuators and manufacturing supply chain are receiving genuine dual-use demand, but tenders and training-ground tests do not prove autonomous combat capability. The US remains stronger in parts of the foundation-model, mission-software and military-validation stack; China’s clearer lead is manufacturing scale and data-acquisition speed.

**Why Robin should care:** Physical AI is expanding from civilian factories into state procurement, but capital must distinguish machines being purchased from machines completing reliable autonomous work.

**One Action:** Apply a dual-use deployment gate tracking delivered contracts, useful field hours, task success, human intervention, endurance, damage and human authorization for force; upgrade no commercial rating from demonstrations, plans or tender values alone.

**Why Robin should care:** Tokenized Bitcoin depends on reserves being provable, redeemable and recoverable. Frozen collateral cannot be underwritten as ordinary liquid capital.

**One Action:** Classify Liquid market-structure impairment / ETF capital direction unchanged; count neither L‑BTC reserves nor associated liquidity as available until the funds return, reserves are re-audited, the Elements defect receives a public post-mortem and independent review, and peg-outs reopen.

## 4. Payments and Token Rails | China rewrites automotive supplier payments by turning the payment clock into an auditable rule

Date: Notice dated September 2 and published September 7, 2026｜Sources: [MIIT/SAMR notice](https://app.xinhuanet.com/news/article.html?articleId=202609079778d500a3b7410ca875293f2fd9e1ae)⁠, [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/china-issues-stricter-supplier-payment-rules-automakers-2026-09-07/)⁠

**Fact:** The rules generally require automotive components to be accepted within three working days of receipt, or five days when vehicle installation must be verified. They encourage payment to SME suppliers within 30 days and no later than 60, restrict buyers from coercing suppliers into commercial paper or electronic receivable instruments, and introduce semiannual and annual payment reporting. Price negotiations generally require interim payment of at least 90% of the latest contract price.

**Inference:** The architectural change covers acceptance, clock commencement, payment medium, discount cost and reporting—not merely faster transfer. Several provisions remain encouragements, so enforcement and actual DSO improvement are unproven. If implemented, supplier financing could migrate from opaque buyer-imposed paper toward explicit bank credit and cash management.

**Why Robin should care:** Eight years in payments reveal that B2B economics often depend less on the rail than on who determines acceptance, when the clock starts and who absorbs financing cost.

**One Action:** Build a B2B payment truth table for automakers covering acceptance time, clock commencement, cash-versus-paper mix, discount-cost allocation, disputes and actual DSO; require two reporting periods before concluding that supplier cash flow structurally improved.

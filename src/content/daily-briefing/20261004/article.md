## 1. Frontier Models and Agents | An always-on agent needs a visible boundary

Date: September 29, 2026 release; official documentation reopened October 4. Seven-day unreported selection.

**Fact:** OpenAI documents Dots as Astra-powered agents with a cloud computer that can keep working while a user’s devices are off. Its proactive research is read-only; follow-up actions require applicable permissions, and rollout is gradual. [dots](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots) [codex](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog) [openai](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog)

**Inference:** Persistence changes the operating model more directly than a benchmark score. A useful evaluation must count accepted work, intervention and recovery within a defined permission boundary.

**Why Robin should care:** RobinOS can compare a persistent research-to-draft workflow with its existing process without assuming a new model or account entitlement.

**One Action:** Save a 48-hour read-only comparison specification with accepted drafts, human minutes, recovery outcomes and attempted boundary crossings; leave execution and results unmeasured.

## 2. Physical AI and Robotics | Momenta’s fleet target needs an operating denominator

Date: Interview September 29; Reuters report September 30, 2026. Seven-day unreported selection.

**Fact:** Momenta told Reuters it has more than 100 robotaxis across three countries and targets several thousand by the end of 2027. Testing locations and fleet targets do not establish paid driverless service or profitability. [momenta](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Mercedes-backed%2BMomenta%2Btargets%2BDubai%2Band%2BEurope%2Bfor%2Brobotaxi%2Bexpansion/27124143.html)

**Inference:** Fleet growth becomes economically meaningful when useful miles and the human support needed to deliver them are visible.

**Why Robin should care:** The Physical AI thesis should distinguish vehicles deployed from service delivered, including the cost of remote assistance.

**One Action:** Save a Momenta operating-evidence card requiring paid driverless miles, interventions per distance and fully loaded cost per mile before treating the fleet target as commercial scale.

## 6. AI Infrastructure, Career and Capital | Who takes the expensive power contract

Date: October 2, 2026 utility announcement. Seven-day unreported selection.

**Fact:** We Energies says Oracle will subscribe to part of its Point Beach energy purchases and estimates about $300 million in customer fuel-cost savings. Full details are to be submitted to Wisconsin’s regulator for review. [energy](https://news.we-energies.com/we-energies-agreement-with-oracle-expected-to-save-customers-approximately-300-million/)

**Inference:** The useful underwriting question is how costs move between the utility, other customers and the data-center buyer if demand or delivery changes. The estimate is not realized savings or regulatory approval.

**Why Robin should care:** Infrastructure judgment requires reading risk allocation beneath headline megawatts. This announcement does not establish an open job or completed facility.

**One Action:** Save a contract-review card for the regulatory filing, with subscription term, volume, termination liability and ratepayer protection left unknown until disclosed.

## 1. Frontier models and OPC autonomy | Remembering how to work, not just what you said

Date: 18 November 2026

**Fact:** Perplexity describes automatically created and updated personal skills: proposed changes undergo deterministic checks, with sampled proposals reviewed by a model. Built-in changes additionally require paired replay and human review. Its small offline experiment used just sixteen paired instances, improving success from 43.8% to 62.5%; that does not establish general autonomous learning. [Original research](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/blog/self-improving-skills).

**Inference / Why Robin should care:** RobinOS’s durable advantage could lie in retained, testable procedural memory. The valuable architecture is proposal, validation and activation—not turning one lucky success into a permanent rule.

**One Action:** Compare one recurring RobinOS workflow before and after a skill revision on identical historical tasks, retaining the revision only if quality, cost and intervention improve overall.

[Official release](https://www.aptiv.com/en/newsroom/article/aptiv-introduces-next-generation-adas-platform-purpose-built-for-the-ai-era).

**Inference / Why Robin should care:** This pass verified no new operating evidence within seven days sufficient to replace already-covered cases, so PRISM remains a watchpoint. Supply-chain integration, validation costs and delivered vehicles will explain US–China competitiveness better than platform branding.

**One Action:** Require a named production program with system costs, operating domain and driver-intervention data before accepting the cost claim.

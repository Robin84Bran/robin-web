How to read P, Q and the intervals / 如何阅读 P、Q 与概率区间

P uses historical returns adjusted for volatility. Q extracts risk-neutral probabilities from option prices, including risk premiums. Q baseline uses the forward and near-ATM volatility without the full smile. Complexity is not proof of accuracy. / P 使用波动率调整后的历史收益；Q 从期权价格提取包含风险溢价的风险中性概率；Q baseline 只用远期与接近平值的波动率。复杂不等于准确。

The median splits probability in half. Central 80% means 10% below and 10% above; central 95% means 2.5% in each tail. These are ending-price ranges, not promises or probabilities of touching a level along the way. Area under the curve is probability; its height is density. / 中位数将概率分成两半。80% 区间外上下各占 10%；95% 区间外各占 2.5%。这是期末价格分布，不是承诺或途中触价概率。曲线下面积是概率，高度是密度。
